# Research basis

## Paper 1 — Agricultural bioenergy supply-chain network design
The first pillar is multi-period, multi-product agricultural supply-chain network design under uncertainty, coupling conventional corn products with biofuel from corn residues. The methodological signature is multi-objective mathematical programming, scenario-based robust optimization, augmented epsilon-constraint, and Lagrangian relaxation, with cost and energy-consumption objectives.

## Paper 2 — Oilseed HEFA-SAF strategic planning
The second pillar is spatial, multi-period strategic planning of an oilseed-based HEFA sustainable aviation fuel supply chain. Its signature is feedstock allocation across Carinata, Camelina and constrained Canola, biorefinery activation and investment timing, airport-level demand, lifecycle emissions, deterministic cost-emission Pareto analysis by epsilon-constraint, and two-stage stochastic programming for demand uncertainty.

## Combined academic identity
The radar therefore prioritizes research at the intersection of renewable fuels/bioenergy, agricultural or biomass feedstocks, supply-chain network design, facility/infrastructure planning, mixed-integer optimization, uncertainty, and sustainability. A page that is only about renewable energy, only about generic supply chains, or only about optimization should not receive an elite score without overlap across these dimensions.
