# Research basis used by the radar

The scoring model is anchored to two academic pillars.

## Paper 1 — Agricultural bioenergy supply-chain network design

Core signals:

- agricultural / agri-food supply chains
- biomass and agricultural residues
- biofuel / bioethanol / bioenergy
- multi-period and multi-product network design
- MILP / mathematical optimisation
- scenario-based robust optimisation and uncertainty
- cost and energy objectives
- augmented epsilon-constraint
- Lagrangian relaxation / decomposition

## Paper 2 — HEFA-SAF strategic planning

Core signals:

- sustainable aviation fuel (SAF)
- HEFA and oilseed feedstocks
- Carinata / Camelina / Canola
- spatial and multi-period supply-chain planning
- agricultural nodes, biorefineries and airport demand
- refinery location, capacity and investment timing
- lifecycle emissions / carbon intensity / land use
- multi-objective epsilon-constraint / Pareto frontier
- two-stage stochastic programming under demand uncertainty

## Shared academic trajectory

The radar interprets the strongest common trajectory as:

**Design and optimisation of agricultural / renewable-fuel supply-chain networks under uncertainty, using operations-research methods to balance economic, energy, environmental and long-term infrastructure decisions.**

## Separate next-paper frontier

The Command Center adds a separate novelty score. It does not redefine the two-paper Research Fit. It rewards projects that extend the trajectory toward:

- multistage/adaptive or distributionally robust optimisation
- joint demand/yield/price/policy uncertainty
- staged/modular capacity expansion
- multimodal freight and transport-mode choice
- broader SAF technology portfolios (HEFA, FT, ATJ, PtL/e-fuels)
- hydrogen/energy-system integration
- harmonised LCA, carbon policy and land-use effects
- circular agricultural bioeconomy
- decomposition algorithms for large national-scale models
